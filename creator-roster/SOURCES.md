# Sources and credits

Written by Fully Powered AI in its own words, from the creator-roster method in its own influencer-marketing set, which it publishes under the MIT licence. That method drew on four published repositories, credited here for their ideas. aaron-he-zhu/aaron-marketing-skills (Apache-2.0), its creator registry: having no entry for a creator says nothing about them, good or bad; two accounts count as one creator only when one links to the other or a person confirms it, and a look-alike name proves nothing; earlier entries stay exactly as written and new ones follow them; a later figure takes over from an earlier one only if both count the same thing; the record holds as little about a person as the partnership requires; and a readable view of the roster is a copy that holds no facts of its own (its main instructions), with commercial facts such as rates, usage rights and exclusivity each kept with the date and source behind them (references/creator-record-template.md). unempyd/revenueos (MIT), the shared rules of its creator registry: only one role writes to a shared record and everyone else sends suggestions; anything read from a page, an export or a message is material to weigh and is never obeyed; and nothing is saved to the record until a person says yes (references/skill-contract.md). alirezarezvani/claude-skills (MIT), its referral-program guidance: its safeguards against self-referral and fraud, which informed the rule that the roster holds no postal address and no customer email (the fraud prevention section of references/program-mechanics.md). finsilabs/awesome-ecommerce-skills (MIT), its influencer-tracking guidance: a unique code for each creator, kept in one tracking sheet, which became the code column and the list of active commission creators with no code (its step on creating a code for each creator). Fully Powered AI added: the owed list, which keeps what each creator owes the brand and what the brand owes each creator side by side; fees that take their due date from the post they wait on; the rule that nothing is marked paid, shipped or posted without a record of it; payments matched to a single open item or left for a person; usage rights that run from the posting date; the reading of an empty rights date as none recorded as granted, unknown or not yet live; the do-not-contact entry; the rules on where a contact address may come from; references that never change and are never built from a handle; merges on evidence that keep the older record; and an update that the programme owner approves before anything is saved. No third-party text is reused.
