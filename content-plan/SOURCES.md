That guide is Fully Powered AI's rewrite of the content strategy method in coreyhaines31/marketingskills (MIT), credited here for these ideas: asking what time and formats the team has, taking subjects from the questions buyers raise in sales conversations and support, keeping each question in the buyer's own words with where it was heard, counting how often a question comes up as a sign of its priority, placing each question at a stage of the buyer's journey that runs past the purchase, telling pieces meant to be found from pieces meant to be passed on, stating what a piece adds to what already exists before it is planned, rating candidates on a few tests before ranking them, cutting one piece into shorter ones for other channels, and treating the place the company owns as where readers end up. Fully Powered AI's own: sizing the plan to the hours a person gives, piece by piece, and stopping when they run out, one owned place only, the limit on what each piece must never say, the test of whether a piece can be said now from the company's own facts, the four places a candidate can land with a reason for each, the single question asked on a first conversation as the measure, and the coverage line.
