# Sources and credits

Written by Fully Powered AI in its own words, from its own Brand Workshop, which it publishes in full under the MIT licence as the brand-workshop repository. The method is not original to Fully Powered AI. The Brand Workshop was built by studying a brand strategy course for young companies and the works that course draws on. The course is private; it is not named here and none of its text is reused. The ideas belong to their authors: "The Red Papers: What's The big ideaL?" (Ogilvy & Mather, 2010), for purpose as a tension met by the company at its best; "Positioning: The Battle for Your Mind" (Al Ries and Jack Trout), for positioning and category; the for, who, is, that, unlike form of the positioning statement, widely credited to Geoffrey A. Moore's "Crossing the Chasm"; "Dimensions of Brand Personality" (Jennifer L. Aaker, Journal of Marketing Research, 1997), for the five dimensions; "Finding Language/Market Fit" (Matt Lerner), for customers' own words, "Now you can ..." and the five-second test; "How Great Leaders Inspire Action" (Simon Sinek, 2009), for starting with why; and two First Round Review articles setting out the advice of the course's teacher, "Great Startups Deserve Great Brands" and "Positioning Your Startup is Vital, Here's How to Nail It", for the order of the three parts, the nested audience, the three kinds of category, traits with an edge and the three-month check. No third-party text is reused beyond the names of those ideas.
