# Brand strategy workshop

Give your company one answer to why it exists, who it is for and how it sounds. Over about three hours (a planning figure, not yet timed), the owner makes the six choices that define the brand from options an AI assistant drafts, signs off the rest, and leaves with the tests that check it with real buyers.

*Workflow template · Marketing and content · version 0.1.0 · [Workflows Directory](https://fullypowered.ai/workflow templates/brand-strategy-workshop/)*

## The outcome

Everyone at your company says the same thing about it: on the website, in an introduction, in a sales conversation and when hiring. You hold one short document with your purpose, the buyer you are going after for the next eighteen months, the category you play in, a positioning statement, five personality traits and a voice guide. You can brief a copywriter, a designer or an agency from it the same afternoon.

Nothing goes in without you. At each of the six decisions the assistant drafts options that differ in substance and says what each one would commit the company to. The owner picks one or writes a better line. The parts the assistant drafts around those decisions, such as the audience and the voice guide, wait for the owner to confirm or correct them. The document also says plainly which lines real buyers have confirmed and which are still waiting for that, so a good-sounding sentence never passes for a proven one.

## Who starts the work, and when

A founder or owner starts it, often with a marketing lead running the session beside them. Start when people at the company describe it in different ways, before a new website or a first campaign, before briefing outside help, or when a new line of business needs its own position.

This is the long one in the directory. Plan on about three hours of the owner's attention: roughly an hour each for purpose, positioning and personality, in one half-day or three sittings. That is a planning figure. Nobody has timed it yet. Testing with buyers follows over the next two or three weeks, and a final check of the team comes three months later.

## What they supply

- What the company sells and to whom.
- How customers handle the problem today, without you.
- Why the founders started the company.
- Customers' own words: reviews, interview notes, sales or support notes. Without them the workshop still runs, and the lines that depend on them are marked as untested.
- Optional: your current website copy, competitors' names and copy, any existing values, mission or brand document, and where you want the documents kept.
- Who is in the session, and which of them decides. The assistant records that only as you tell it.

## What comes out

1. **A brand strategy.** One document, answer first: purpose, who it is for, category, positioning statement, personality, voice, lines ready to use, and what is not yet validated.
2. **A choices record.** Each of the six decisions the owner made, the options that were on the table, the owner's words, and what the owner confirmed or corrected.
3. **A validation kit.** A two-sentence spoken test that a buyer tells back to you, an interview guide, a five-second reading test and a three-month team check, filled in for your company and ready for you to run.

A good result reads like your company and nobody else's. A competitor could not sign the positioning statement with the names changed, and a new hire could repeat the purpose after reading it once.

## What has to change for your company

Decide who makes the choices: one owner, or founders who agree to settle each one together in the room. Gather your customers' real words first, because they make the difference between a tested line and a hopeful one. Supply your competitors' current copy if you want the distinctiveness check run. Use your own word for what you build. Choose where the document lives and who may revise it, and pick the AI product you run it in: ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot or Gemini.

The workshop stops at words. Logo, colour, type and naming are separate work, and the brand strategy is the brief for them.

## Start using it

1. Read [EXAMPLE.md](EXAMPLE.md) to see what a good result looks like.
2. Open [TEMPLATE.md](TEMPLATE.md) and set every item under "What to customize" to your own rules, roles and limits.
3. Give your AI assistant the instruction block and the output format from the template, with your own limits in place of the ones the instructions leave open in square brackets. Paste them in as custom instructions in ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot or Gemini, or copy this whole folder into a product that loads `SKILL.md` packages. This folder stands alone; you do not need the rest of the repository.
4. Run it on past work first, using the reviewer checklist and acceptance tests in the template, before anyone relies on it.
