Fully Powered AI's own: the short note of your facts as the only source for anything the magnet says; grouping buyer questions in their own words, with every source line and a count, and choosing the question by that count; one reader named by role; the verdict that no magnet should be made, with its reason; checking the chosen format against the hours you gave; the five checks as written here; the limits taken from what you must never say; the no-form rule until an email list and a follow-up exist; the wording of the first-contact question and recording its answer where leads are kept, with no targets; and the rule that the assistant writes, builds, publishes and sends nothing. No third-party text is reused.
