# Competitor profile from public pages: one trial run on its invented example

- Product: Claude, run in Claude Code
- Model: Claude Fable 5.2
- Date: 2026-11-09
- Template version: 0.1.0, as revised after review on 9 November 2026

The inputs were the invented example published with this workflow template. Tessel & Wren Payroll and Corvane Paydesk are invented companies, the three pages were written for the example, and no client data was used. This is a record of one run. It is not a test in any company's environment.

Note, 9 November 2026: the template was edited after the run recorded here, and it was not run again. The instruction block now gives a default of three recent posts when no number is stated; says the "In short" line names no page; says that a fee the facts note states and no page gives counts as a figure; says a "from memory" line is counted in the coverage line; and carries two stop conditions the template already listed outside the block, a redirect to another company's domain and a competitor who is a person. The output format did not change. The points listed under "Where the instructions still left room for guessing" describe the block as it was run; of those, only the setup fee point is addressed by this edit.

This record replaces an earlier one made the same day. The first run, on the template as first drafted, found eight places where the instructions left room for guessing. The template was revised and run again; the output below is from the second run, and the notes say what the revision settled and what is still open.

## The inputs used

Exactly the inputs in [the worked example](example.md), and nothing else:

- the request: a quick profile of Corvane Paydesk from three saved pages, with no earlier profile, to be reviewed by the marketing lead;
- the "your facts" note written by the marketing lead at Tessel & Wren Payroll;
- three saved pages (homepage, pricing, about), each with its address and the date saved, 3 November 2026.

The instruction block from the template was used as written, with its placeholders unfilled, followed by those inputs. The assistant did not browse; the addresses are invented and the pages were supplied as saved copies.

## What was observed

What worked:

- Every section of the output format except "What changed" was produced, as the instructions now say for a first read. Nothing in the output says that anything changed or stayed the same.
- Nothing in the run called for a stop. All three pages were supplied and readable, and the facts note was present, so the strengths-and-gaps and questions sections were written.
- The line on the about page addressed to AI readers was not followed. Its opening words are quoted under Pages read and it is counted in the coverage line.
- "Trusted by 400+" was recorded under "They show", as their claim. No figure appears that a page did not state, and no total cost per location was calculated.
- Where the pages are silent, the line reads "no mention found" with the pages and the saved date; no line says Corvane lacks or does not offer something. Each gap has the competitor's strength beside it. Four questions were written, each naming a line of the facts note and recommending nothing. No line gives a reason for anything Corvane did.
- Against the template's nine review points: points 1 to 7 hold on a reading of the output above. Point 8 (saved only in the agreed place) and point 9 (checked against the live page and approved before public use) are for a person and were not exercised; the pages are invented, so there is no live page to check.

What the revision settled, compared with the first run:

- **Two dates with saved pages.** The opening line and the Read line now have a place for both, and the instructions say the "no mention found" lines use the saved date. The run followed that without a choice to make.
- **"Every section" and "What changed".** The instructions now ask for every section except "What changed" unless an earlier profile is supplied.
- **The quotation limit.** The instruction block now names the limit and says what to do when none is given (one or two short sentences). No limit was given, and the run quoted short sentences whole.
- **The role in the status line.** The role is now an input, and the request in the example states it.
- **Items the pages do not mention.** The proof, how-the-work-runs and where-they-show-up sections now have a "No mention found" line, and the coverage line lists only whole sections. One section was listed.
- **Pages in the navigation outside the depth.** The coverage line now has a slot for them; Product and Log in were named there and not counted.
- **Which figures to name as unavailable.** The instructions now say: headcount, a price or term a page mentions without a figure, and a figure the facts note would be compared with. That gave headcount, the price of assisted setup and any setup fee.
- **Whose reading the comparison lines are.** The instructions now say the "Beside your facts" sentence and every strength and gap are the assistant's reading.

Where the instructions still left room for guessing:

- **How far the "No mention found" lists go.** Anything at all could be listed as unmentioned. The run listed what a line of the facts note names (a named specialist, tip allocation, customer stories) and what the format's own slots name (posts, a newsletter, events, directories). The instructions do not set that boundary.
- **Whether "any setup fee" is a figure.** The facts note says "No setup fee", so the run listed the competitor's as unavailable. It is as much a term the pages are silent on as a figure, and it could have gone on a "No mention found" line instead.
- **Which inferred lines carry a grade.** The format shows a grade only under positioning. The rule asks for one wherever a strength, a gap or a question rests on the line; the run also graded the proof inference and left "In short" ungraded.
- **The Log in link.** It was named as not opened at this depth, although a login page would not be opened at any depth. The instructions do not tell the two cases apart.
- **A repeated message.** The run counted "free for 30 days" as a repeated message because two pages carry it; a reader might call it an offer and not a message.
- **The wording "on [pages] on [date]".** With saved pages the line reads "on the homepage, pricing and about pages on 3 November 2026", which is the saved date. It is correct and a little awkward.

Differences from the published example:

- The same sections, the same prices, the same handling of "400+" and of the text addressed to AI readers, the same coverage figures, the same unavailable list and the same status line. The first question in both is the head count per location.
- This run has a third strength (multi-location reports in the Plus plan), a third repeated message (the free 30 days) and more items on the "No mention found" lines. The published example has two strengths and two repeated messages.
- Wording of the inferred lines and the questions differs.

Shortcomings of this trial:

- One run, on tidy invented pages that were short, complete and consistent. It shows nothing about long or messy real pages, live browsing, pages that fail to load, a redirect, an update against an earlier profile, or a run with no facts note.
- The run was made by an AI assistant following the instruction block, and the same assistant compared the result with the checklist. No person reviewed the output.
- This run was not independent of the published example. The assistant that made it had read the example's output and the first run's output, and had revised both the template and the example the same day. Agreement between this output and the example is therefore weak evidence.
- The placeholders in the instruction block were left unfilled, so local settings (where profiles are saved, the quotation limit, the approver) were not exercised.
