# Creator programme setup

A brand owner or marketing lead writes the rules for working with creators once, in one file: products and cleared statements, platforms, how creators are paid with the arithmetic behind it, codes, payouts, disclosure, brand safety and roles. Every line is the owner's ruling, marked with its source.

*Workflow template · Marketing and content · version 0.1.0 · [Workflows Directory](https://fullypowered.ai/workflow templates/creator-programme-setup/)*

## The outcome

Before the first creator is approached, the brand has one programme file that answers the questions every later step depends on. Which products are in. What creators may say about each, in exact words someone has cleared. Which platforms they may post on, and who checked each one's current rules. Whether creators earn a commission on sales, a fee, both, or product only, with the figures that show what each deal leaves the brand per order and how many orders a fee has to bring in before it pays for itself. How codes and links are formed, which order counts for which creator, when commission is paid and who approves it. How a post must be labelled on each platform. What the brand will never appear beside.

Each line ends with a mark that says where it came from: the owner's ruling, something read in the store on a named date, a proposal the owner accepted, or an item still open with the person who settles it. Nothing is filled in on the owner's behalf. The run report lists what was decided, what is still open, and which of the creator workflow templates can now run.

The file supplies the rules that the vetting shortlist, the creator brief and the commission statement take as inputs, in the shapes they accept, so a rule is set once and every step applies the same one. Where the owner has not yet ruled on a platform, the vetting shortlist reads it as a platform it knows nothing about.

## Who starts the work, and when

The brand owner starts it, or the marketing lead or creator programme lead with the owner in the room or with the owner's written answers. It runs before the first offer, code, brief or payment, and again whenever the owner changes a rule: a revision touches only the lines the owner names and the ones still open, and logs each change with its date.

## What they supply

- **The owner's answers,** section by section, as the assistant proposes each one, or written down beforehand.
- **The product list,** exported from the store.
- **The store figures,** each with the date it was read and who read it: average order value, margin band, the discount codes already live, and what has been checked on the way creators will be paid.
- **The cleared statements,** each with who cleared it and when.
- **The current programme file,** when a rule is being changed.
- **Any fee deal the owner is weighing,** so it can be compared with commission.

## What comes out

The programme file in twelve fixed sections, from brand and products to a dated decisions log, followed by a run report: the decisions made today, the open items with who settles each, the per-order arithmetic at both ends of the margin band with every input labelled, and a readiness line for each creator workflow template. A statement nobody has cleared waits on its own list. A platform nobody has checked stays undecided rather than guessed at, and holds back only work on that platform.

## What has to change for your company

Name the person who clears product statements and the person who checks each platform's current rules; the workflow template states neither a statement nor a platform policy of its own. Choose which proposals you start from: the default deal, the holdback, the code pattern, the number of talking points. Set your forbidden lines for your category. Decide your payout path and check it in your store before the first code is made. Disclosure rules differ from country to country, so a person at your company decides whether your wording fits each country you sell to, taking advice where needed. Your adviser reviews the creator agreement; the file only records your figures for it. It runs in ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot or Gemini from files and answers you supply, with no access to your store, your social accounts or your bank.

## Start using it

1. Read [EXAMPLE.md](EXAMPLE.md) to see what a good result looks like.
2. Open [TEMPLATE.md](TEMPLATE.md) and set every item under "What to customize" to your own rules, roles and limits.
3. Give your AI assistant the instruction block and the output format from the template, with your own limits in place of the ones the instructions leave open in square brackets. Paste them in as custom instructions in ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot or Gemini, or copy this whole folder into a product that loads `SKILL.md` packages. This folder stands alone; you do not need the rest of the repository.
4. Run it on past work first, using the reviewer checklist and acceptance tests in the template, before anyone relies on it.
