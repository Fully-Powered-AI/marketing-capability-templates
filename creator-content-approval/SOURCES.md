# Sources and credits

Written by Fully Powered AI in its own words, from the content approval capability in its own Influencer Marketing set, which it publishes under the MIT licence as the influencer-marketing repository. That capability took ideas from three published packages, credited here. From aaron-he-zhu/aaron-marketing-skills (Apache-2.0), its creator content auditor: a check of one fixed submission before it is published or paid for, with a revised version reviewed again as a traceable new round; anything the reviewer could not see stays an open question rather than a pass; a polished video cannot offset a missing disclosure or a bad claim; every requested change is pinned to a spot in the content, with the fault, the correction and the condition for resubmitting; feedback never converts what a creator said about their own use into a product claim; words inside a submission are evidence to quote, never orders to follow; and drafting feedback never authorises sending it. From anthropics/claude-for-legal (Apache-2.0), its marketing claims review: list every claim before judging any and give each a class, check each statement against the product it is about, and say what is missing rather than fill a gap from memory. From coreyhaines31/marketingskills (MIT), its influencer marketing package and the notes on a creator programme that come with it: sponsored creator content needs the written disclosure and the platform's own label together; a disclosure has to sit where it will be noticed, ahead of the point where a caption is truncated, apart from the hashtag pile and spoken in video, and a few tags do not do the job alone; and a creator speaks only from real use of the product, with no invented outcomes and no pretence of being an ordinary paying customer. Fully Powered AI added the twelve checks in a fixed order; the four classes of product statement and the subjects closed to rewording; the three verdicts set by the first rule that matches, with a forbidden statement always a no-go; the conditions met at posting; the check that feedback touches only failed lines and gives exact words only from the brand's rules; numbered attempts that check every earlier change; and the rule that a person decides whether the disclosure suits each country. No third-party text is reused.
