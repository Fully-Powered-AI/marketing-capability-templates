# Creator content approval: an invented example

*Illustrative example with invented data.* Brackenfold Tea Company, its teas, its people, the creator, the code and the web address below are all invented. First come the inputs the brand's creator programme lead supplied, in full, then the review the workflow template returned.

The example shows what a reader should look for. One forbidden health statement makes the verdict a NO-GO, even though most of the post is fine. A cleared statement said in the creator's own sentence still counts, because it carries the cleared words. A paraphrase of a cleared line is asked back to the exact words. A statement nobody has ruled on goes to the founder as a question. The disclosure at the end of the caption, and missing from the video, gets a change item each. A covering note claiming an approval is quoted and not followed. The creator's opinions are left alone.

## The inputs

### 1. Brackenfold's creator rules (dated 12 October 2026)

```
Brand: Brackenfold Tea Company
Countries the creators' audiences are in: United Kingdom, Ireland
Clearing role: founder
Disclosure role: founder
Sender role: creator programme lead
We reply to drafts within: 3 working days

Cleared statements
- Harbour Breakfast tea: "Single-estate Assam, picked in the second flush." cleared by founder, 2026-10-12
- Harbour Breakfast tea: "Brews strong enough to take milk." cleared by founder, 2026-10-12
- Harbour Breakfast tea: "Packed in home-compostable pouches." cleared by founder, 2026-10-12
- Lantern Mint tea: "Caffeine-free." cleared by founder, 2026-10-12

Forbidden statements
- Any statement that a tea treats, prevents or eases a condition or a
  symptom, for example "detox", "settles your stomach", "boosts
  immunity": we hold no evidence, and it is a health claim.
- Comparisons with a named brand: we do not compare ourselves by name.
- "organic": our estates are not certified.
- Results or experiences the creator has not had.

Closed categories: health or body effects; comparisons with a named
brand; certifications, test results and awards.

Platform rulings
- instagram: yes
- tiktok: with conditions: no trending sounds that carry lyrics
- youtube: undecided

Disclosure
- instagram: "#ad" | at the very start of the caption; in a Reel, said
  aloud and shown on screen in the first three seconds; the
  paid-partnership label switched on as well | spoken: this is an ad
  for Brackenfold
- tiktok: "#ad" | first line of the caption; said aloud in the first
  three seconds; the branded-content setting switched on | spoken: this
  is an ad for Brackenfold
- Words that do not count on their own: "#spon", "#partner", "#gifted",
  "thanks to Brackenfold"
- Caption window: 100 characters

Audience rule: made for adults; nobody who appears under 18; nothing
aimed at children; no age setting required.
Redlines: no alcohol in shot; no political slogans; nothing that mocks a
person or a group.
```

Opinions left alone: "This is my favourite slow-morning tea by a mile." (caption line 1); "which is exactly how I like it" (0:07); "so I feel good about my morning cup" (0:12).

**Why**

- statements: "detox" is a forbidden health statement, so this version cannot run; one paraphrase and one statement awaiting a ruling as well.
- disclosure-position: "#ad" is at the end of the caption, far past the 100-character window.
- disclosure-in-video: the disclosure is neither said nor shown in the video.

Changes asked last time: none: first attempt.
