# Sources and credits

Written by Fully Powered AI in its own words, from the creator outreach method in its own Influencer Marketing set, which it publishes under the MIT licence as the influencer-marketing repository. That method took ideas from three published repositories, credited here. From aaron-he-zhu/aaron-marketing-skills (Apache-2.0), its outreach manager: a first message followed by a short run of follow-ups, each adding something new, getting shorter and making a no easy, with a final note that closes politely; a short version for direct messages and a formal one for a manager; and a decline that ends one campaign's messages kept apart from a request never to be contacted again (influencer/activate/outreach-manager, its main instructions). From the same package's cold copy rules: never opening on the sender, opening instead on the creator's name and a dated, visible thing they made, never claiming a history with the creator that the sender does not have, no link in the first message, and a small question as the ask (influencer/activate/outreach-manager/references/cold-copy-rules.md). From the same repository's contract helper: the line between what outreach may discuss and what the written agreement settles, so that terms are left to the agreement (influencer/activate/contract-helper). From unifapi-agent/agents (MIT), its port of the KOL Pricing framework by Antonia (Antoniaiaiaiaia/kol-pricing, MIT): the engage, negotiate and skip handling, in which a creator marked skip is approached only when asked for, and then only with the basic deal; and a message that refers to one recent post, stays within a set length, carries no hype, emoji or exclamation marks and ends on a low-effort ask (skills/influencer-marketing-agent/kol-pricing, its main instructions). From coreyhaines31/marketingskills (MIT): approaching creators individually rather than in bulk, with each note pointing to the creator's own work, the reason the brand chose them and the benefit to their followers (skills/influencer-marketing, its outreach section). Fully Powered AI added: the four versions keyed to how each creator can be reached; the stops for a creator who is excluded, asked not to be contacted, may have an audience under 18 or has no known way to be reached; the rule that a message states the standard offer as the brand gives it and nothing the written agreement settles; follow-up facts that are terms held back for a person; no product statement unless a named person cleared it, word for word; nothing about a creator's body, health, sex life or identity; the ten-point self-check, the word bands and the default spacing of 4, 5 and 7 days. No third-party text is reused.

- **Fully Powered AI:** The creator outreach method in Fully Powered AI's Influencer Marketing set (Fully-Powered-AI/influencer-marketing, MIT): a first message built from the one thing the creator made, why them, what their audience gets, the offer in a line and one easy question; channel versions; three follow-ups then stop; vetting verdicts respected; no product statements in outreach; and a person who reads and sends every message.
