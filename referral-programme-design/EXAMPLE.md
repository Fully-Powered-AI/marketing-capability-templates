# Referral programme design: an invented example

*Illustrative example with invented data.* The practice, its owner, the clients, the adviser and every word quoted below are invented. An invented 11-person bookkeeping and payroll practice, here called Practice K, wins most of its new clients by recommendation. Its owner, Priya, wants to stop leaving that to chance.

The inputs come first, in full, so the template can be run on them. The programme follows.

## The inputs

### Your facts

```
Business: Practice K, an 11-person bookkeeping and payroll practice.
Owner: Priya
What we do: monthly bookkeeping, payroll and year-end clean-ups for
  owner-run businesses.
The client we want more of: trade and service businesses with 5 to 50
  staff that still run payroll from spreadsheets.
First step for anyone new: a 30-minute call with Priya, then a written
  note of what we would take on and when.
How to reach us: priya@practice-k.example
What we give back: a thank-you, and an introduction in return where we
  can make a useful one. No fees, discounts or gifts.
Our profession's rules on referral rewards: not read; no reward planned.
Sign-off: Priya
Introductions are recorded in: the client spreadsheet, tab "Introductions".
Review: every quarter.
Settings: the defaults. Today: 9 November 2026.
```

Every client, adviser and contact named in the programme is on the list, and every quotation can be found word for word in the row it cites. Nothing was sent and no record was changed.
