# Referral programme design

Turn the recommendations your work already earns into a habit: when to ask, who on your own list to ask first and for what, what you give back, the words for each ask and the note an introducer forwards, and how every introduction is recorded. You send each message yourself.

*Workflow template · Marketing and content · version 0.1.0 · [Workflows Directory](https://fullypowered.ai/workflows/referral-programme-design/)*

## The outcome

Your best clients already talk about you. This workflow template makes sure you ask them, at the right moment, in words that sound like you. You get one programme for your business: the moments when an ask belongs and the ones when it never does, the people on your list to ask first, the people to leave alone for now and why, and what you give back.

The words come ready to send: the ask to a client, the ask to an adviser who serves the same clients, a question for someone you have only spoken with, a short note about you for an introducer to pass on, one follow-up and the thank-you. Every line about a person comes from your own list or is left for you to fill in, so nothing puts words in a client's mouth. Each introduction is then recorded the same way, so at every review you know where your new work came from.

## Who starts the work, and when

The owner starts it, or whoever keeps the client list: when you decide referrals should stop being luck, then again at each review to refresh who to ask. Professional services firms come first, from bookkeeping and design to engineering and IT support, wherever new clients arrive because someone vouched for you.

The owner approves the programme and sends every message, from their own account, when they choose. The workflow template contacts no one.

## What they supply

- **Your facts.** A few lines: what you do and for whom, the kind of client you want more of, the first step anyone new takes, what you are willing to give back, your sign-off, and where you keep a record of introductions.
- **Your relationship list.** A spreadsheet or export of clients, former clients, advisers and contacts, with what each person has seen of your work, when, and how it went, in their words where you have them. If your column headers differ from the defaults, the assistant asks which is which before it starts.
- **Your settings,** if you want to change the defaults: how many people to ask first, how long to wait before the one follow-up, and how long to leave someone after they last introduced you.

## What comes out

One programme in a fixed order, as a draft for you to approve:

1. **In short:** who to ask first, at which moments, and what you give back.
2. **When to ask,** and the never-cases.
3. **Who to ask first,** each with what they have seen, what to ask them for and how to open, using their own words where your list has them.
4. **Not asked now,** each with the reason and when to ask again.
5. **What you give back,** to the person who introduces you and to the person introduced.
6. **The words,** with your facts filled in.
7. **How to record an introduction,** and what to count at each review.
8. **Where each line comes from,** and what is left for you to decide.

See the worked example with invented data in [EXAMPLE.md](EXAMPLE.md).

## What has to change for your company

- **Your list.** One row per person, with how the work went in the client's own words where you have them. Map your column headers to the defaults once.
- **Your moments.** The defaults are work in use with a pleased client, a good first conversation and a catch-up with an adviser. Add the moments your work has, such as the end of year-end work.
- **What you give back.** Thanks and introductions in return are the starting point. If you ever consider a reward, read your profession's rules first. The template gives no view on them and proposes none.
- **Your record.** Decide where introductions are logged and who keeps that log up to date.
- **Your platform.** It runs in ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot or Gemini. Decide which list the assistant may read.

## Start using it

1. Read [EXAMPLE.md](EXAMPLE.md) to see what a good result looks like.
2. Open [TEMPLATE.md](TEMPLATE.md) and set every item under "What to customize" to your own rules, roles and limits.
3. Give your AI assistant the instruction block and the output format from the template, with your own limits in place of the ones the instructions leave open in square brackets. Paste them in as custom instructions in ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot or Gemini, or copy this whole folder into a product that loads `SKILL.md` packages. This folder stands alone; you do not need the rest of the repository.
4. Run it on past work first, using the reviewer checklist and acceptance tests in the template, before anyone relies on it.
