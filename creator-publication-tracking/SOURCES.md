# Sources and credits

Written by Fully Powered AI in its own words, from the publication tracking capability in its own Influencer Marketing set, which it publishes under the MIT licence as the influencer-marketing repository. That capability took ideas from published packages, credited here for the ones this template uses. From aaron-he-zhu/aaron-marketing-skills (Apache-2.0), the tracker in its campaign planner: a checkpoint for each agreed post from each creator; each observation added as a new dated record rather than written over the last one; anything nobody has evidenced kept as unknown rather than counted as passed; a live post that is missing, changed or different from what was approved sent back to the content reviewer; and the list of what needs attention built from a stated date. From the same repository, the review templates of its creator content auditor: checking the required elements of a post one at a time, such as the disclosure, the link and the promo code; each requested change saying what is there now, what is needed and by when; and drafting a message never authorising sending it. From finsilabs/awesome-ecommerce-skills (MIT), its influencer tracking package: one tracking link and one discount code issued to each creator for each campaign, so the live post can be compared with exactly what was issued, and the note that on some platforms the link sits in the profile rather than the caption. From the same repository, its UGC campaign management package: checking that what a published piece shows and claims matches what was cleared, which became the line comparing each live post with its approved version. Fully Powered AI added the self-contained form that works from a deliverables list, a post log and an actions log a person keeps; the stop when a column header does not match; the disclosure judged as yes, partial or no against the brand's own required forms; the link comparison that ignores only the protocol, a leading www. and a trailing slash; the four headings of overdue, act now, not checked and coming up; the rule that only a fresh look at the post closes an item; the removal referral that quotes the creator's own term without judging the creator; delivery by creator; and the rule that the report sends, posts, pays and changes nothing. No third-party text is reused.
