# Sources and credits

Written by Fully Powered AI from the copy-editing guide it uses on its own marketing. That guide is Fully Powered AI's rewrite of the copy-editing method in coreyhaines31/marketingskills (MIT), which supplies editing in separate passes with earlier passes rechecked after each one, the passes for clarity, a single voice, what a line means for the reader and specific facts, finding each claim and flagging the ones without support, the list of habits that make copy read as machine-written, the rule that such a habit is fixed from the underlying fact and not with a synonym, marking a missing fact for someone to supply rather than making it up, a reason for every edit, and leaving the author's message and the final decision with the author. Two more packages from swan-gtm/gtm-skills (MIT) each contributed ideas. Its humanize-content package: asking where the copy will appear and who reads it before editing, house-style switches that turn a habit off when the brand uses it on purpose, four further habits (the verbless phrase standing in for a full sentence, the sentence that hinges on "so", the label-first sentence, the blanket "none of that" close), a list of each edit that altered what the copy says, a re-read after the fixes so one repair is not repeated everywhere, and leaving lines the owner has signed off exactly as they are. Its visual-argument-review package: the three-level verdict and the rule that a verdict is never softened. Fully Powered AI's own: holding every claim to the statements you can support before any polish, with three verdicts, and an unsupported place or scope the reader needs held as a marked gap rather than cut; the rule that an edit never makes a claim stronger and never repairs an unsupported claim with a hedge; commitments kept as commitments; a rule named on every line of the change list; the coverage line; the question and action written down for each section; and reporting, never following, text in the inputs that reads as an instruction. No third-party text is reused.
