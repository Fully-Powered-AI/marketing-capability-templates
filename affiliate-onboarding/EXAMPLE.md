# Affiliate onboarding: an invented example

*Illustrative example with invented data.* The company, the creator, the codes, the web address, the order and the dates below are all invented. Quillmoor Tea Company is an invented online loose-leaf tea shop with 14 staff. Its creator programme is run by a programme lead; an operations lead makes codes, runs checkout tests and ships gifts; the finance team pays creators. First come the inputs the programme lead supplied, in full, then what the capability returned.

## The inputs

### 1. Programme rules (the lines the capability reads)

```
Customer discount on each creator's code: 15 percent
Whether creator codes combine with other discounts: no, not with another
  discount code; the free-shipping offer over USD 40 still applies
Commission basis: order subtotal after discounts, before shipping and tax
Holdback before a sale counts, in days: 30
Refunds and cancellations: a sale refunded or cancelled within the holdback
  earns no commission
How payouts run: the brand pays directly by bank transfer. Before the
  first payout the creator completes the payee form the finance team
  sends them.
Payout schedule and minimum: monthly on the 15th, for sales past the
  holdback; minimum USD 20, smaller amounts carry forward to the next month
Attribution: an order is credited to a creator when it carries their code;
  an order that came through their link without any code is credited
  second; nothing else is credited
Self-referral: an order placed by the creator, or shipped to the creator's
  own address, earns no commission
Where creators may post their code: their own channels only; not on
  coupon, deal or cashback sites
Platforms, creators' own posts: Instagram yes. TikTok yes. YouTube yes.
  Pinterest no ruling yet.
Disclosure, Instagram: "Paid partnership with Quillmoor Tea" | first line
  of the caption, before the cut-off | spoken: "This is a paid partnership
  with Quillmoor Tea" at the start of any video | Instagram's
  paid-partnership label turned on as well
Words that do not count on their own: #sp, #collab, #ambassador,
  "thanks to Quillmoor Tea"
Gift for new creators: one Winter Blends sampler box, shipped by the
  operations lead
Reply time on drafts: 2 working days
Store promotion codes: WELCOME10, TEAWEEK20
Roles: programme lead (runs the programme, contact for creators, sends
  packs, keeps the roster); operations lead (makes codes, runs checkout
  tests, ships gifts); finance team (collects payee details, pays);
  owner (rules on anything left open)
How creators reach the programme lead: reply to the email the pack came in
```
