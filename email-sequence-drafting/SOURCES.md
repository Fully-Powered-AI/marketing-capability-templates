# Sources and credits

Written by Fully Powered AI in its own words, from the email drafting guide it uses for its own emails. That guide adapts the lifecycle email method in coreyhaines31/marketingskills (MIT), which supplies the welcome, nurture and onboarding shapes, the questions about what started the sequence, what readers already know and what else they receive, a single purpose and a single call to action in each email, something useful before any ask, a first email that delivers what was promised and says what follows, a plain subject that works alone, a preview that adds to the subject without repeating it, merge-field fallbacks, the per-email layout and the list of wording that marks an email as generated. anthropics/knowledge-work-plugins (Apache-2.0) contributed three ideas: settle, before any email is written, which action by the reader means the sequence has worked and can stop; hold back a sequence's emails from anyone in the middle of a different sequence or who has unsubscribed; and say on each email who receives it. Fully Powered AI's own: writing only to people whose own request supports it, keeping the sequence inside what the sign-up promised, a reply that pauses the sequence, a last email and no running on, proposed timing with no benchmarks or rates, the facts page every claim must trace to, the slots for anything missing, and the refusal of invented discounts, deadlines, quotations and last-chance emails. No third-party text is reused.
