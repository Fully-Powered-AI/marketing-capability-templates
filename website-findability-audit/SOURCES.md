# Sources and credits

Written by Fully Powered AI from the search-readiness and AI-discovery guides it uses for its own website. Those guides are its rewrite of the seo-audit and ai-seo methods in coreyhaines31/marketingskills (MIT), credited for the audit's areas in order of reach, then content, the warning that a page copy without its scripts cannot prove structured data is absent, and answer passages that make sense on their own. Further ideas are credited to rampstackco/claude-skills (MIT) for checking reach before content, one address per page across canonical, sitemap and links, and grouping fixes by shared cause with a check that confirms each; to aaron-he-zhu/aaron-marketing-skills (Apache-2.0) for naming where each finding's evidence came from and leaving an unavailable check open rather than guessing; to anthropics/knowledge-work-plugins (Apache-2.0) for refusing text written for machines alone, leaving crawler access to the owner, giving no overall score, promising nothing about what an assistant recommends, and naming what could not be read; and to unifapi-agent/agents (MIT) for treating a published fact the owner has not supplied as an open question rather than an error, and dating each audit so that the same check, repeated later, shows whether a fix was made. Fully Powered AI added the work from saved pages, the line between what was read and what needs a live check, the seventeen checks, the 'your facts' note and the customer questions. No third-party text is reused.
