# Customer case study

With a customer who has agreed to help, prepare the interview, then turn their words and the figures they supply into a case study in a short and a long version. Every quotation and number is listed for the customer to approve in writing, and nothing is published or named before they do.

*Workflow template · Marketing and content · version 0.1.0 · [Workflows Directory](https://fullypowered.ai/workflows/customer-case-study/)*

## The outcome

A customer said yes, and now you have a case study you can stand behind. You walk into the interview with a guide that asks open questions, never leading ones, and that always asks what is still unsolved. You walk out with a draft in two lengths: a short one for an email or a sales conversation, and a long one for your website.

Every sentence in it comes from the customer's interview or from the figures they chose to share. Each quotation is the customer's exact words. Each number shows its before, its after and where it came from. A claim your team would like to make, but the customer never made, sits on a separate list instead of in the story.

The customer sees everything before anyone else does: every quotation, every figure and every detail that could identify them, each ready to approve, change or remove. Until they agree in writing, they appear as a plain description, never by name.

## Who starts the work, and when

The marketer or owner who looks after the customer relationship starts it as soon as a customer has agreed in writing to take part, and runs it a second time once the interview record and the customer's figures are back. Whoever conducts the interview uses the guide. The customer approves the draft, and a named person in your company records that approval and decides where the finished case study appears.

The workflow template prepares. It contacts no one, sends nothing and publishes nothing.

## What they supply

- **The customer's yes, in writing,** and what they agreed to: the interview, recording, whether they may be named, anything to keep out.
- **A short brief:** what you did for them, when, and who the case study is for.
- **The interview record:** a transcript, or notes with the customer's exact words inside quotation marks.
- **The figures sheet** the customer fills in, with a column saying whether each figure may be published.
- **Your settings:** how many results to show, the lengths of the two versions and your closing line.

## What comes out

Two documents, one for each pass:

1. **The interview guide:** what the customer has agreed so far, the facts from your brief to confirm in their words, the questions in timed parts, the figures to ask for, the checks to read out at the end, and the figures sheet to send if you choose.
2. **The draft for the customer's approval:** the short version, the long version, and an approval list with a tag for every quotation, number and identifying detail. Under it: any place where what the customer said and what the figures show disagree, everything left out and why, and the decisions left to you.

Good looks like this: a reader can point at any line of the case study and find its source in the interview or the figures sheet.

## What has to change for your company

- **Who approves.** Name the person who sends the draft to the customer, keeps their written approval and publishes the result.
- **Your figures sheet.** Keep the default columns or change them, and decide what you say when you send it.
- **Your limits.** How many results a case study shows, and how long each version runs.
- **How you describe a customer who is not named,** and the closing line you want at the end.
- **The interview.** Who conducts it, how long it runs and whether you record it.
- **Your platform.** It runs in ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot or Gemini, from records you supply. Decide which records the assistant may read and where drafts are kept.

## Start using it

1. Read [EXAMPLE.md](EXAMPLE.md) to see what a good result looks like.
2. Open [TEMPLATE.md](TEMPLATE.md) and set every item under "What to customize" to your own rules, roles and limits.
3. Give your AI assistant the instruction block and the output format from the template, with your own limits in place of the ones the instructions leave open in square brackets. Paste them in as custom instructions in ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot or Gemini, or copy this whole folder into a product that loads `SKILL.md` packages. This folder stands alone; you do not need the rest of the repository.
4. Run it on past work first, using the reviewer checklist and acceptance tests in the template, before anyone relies on it.
