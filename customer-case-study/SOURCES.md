# Sources and credits

Written by Fully Powered AI in its own words. Two openly licensed packages each contributed ideas. LeadMagic/gtm-skills (MIT), its case study builder: the situation, what was done and results as the backbone of the story, opening with the result, a short version and a full version from the same material, results stated as specific figures with a before and an after, quoting the customer exactly instead of rewriting them in sales language, keeping to a few strong results, and the customer's written approval before anything is published, with the customer free to change their own quotations. naveedharri/benai-skills (MIT), the interview questions of its case study package: an interview taken part by part (the customer, what was happening, what was hard and what it cost, what was done, the results, the quotation, what comes next) and pressing for a before-and-after figure and for when the results first showed. Fully Powered AI's own rules for prepared work, written for this template: the two passes, with the customer's written agreement required before the guide; questions that never offer the answer, and always one about what is still unsolved; facts from the requester's brief used only when the customer says them; the figures sheet with its publish column and the stop when its headers do not match; figures copied as supplied, estimates said to be estimates and worked-out figures shown with their working; spoken figures that disagree with the sheet held back as differences to settle; the tagged approval list of every quotation, number and identifying detail; the plain description in place of a name until naming is agreed; and the Not used list. No third-party text is reused.
