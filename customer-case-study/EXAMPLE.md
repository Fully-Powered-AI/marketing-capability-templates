# Customer case study: an invented example

*Illustrative example with invented data.* Firm F, Client K, the people and every word quoted below are invented. Firm F is an invented nine-person bookkeeping and finance-operations firm. Its owner wants a case study about Client K, an invented heating and cooling installer whose invoicing Firm F took over. Client K's operations director has agreed to help.

The inputs for each pass come first, in full, so the template can be run on them. Each output follows its inputs.

## First pass: the inputs

### The customer's agreement

```
From: operations director, Client K
To: owner, Firm F
Date: 2 October 2026

Happy to do a 30-minute call for your case study. Record it if you like.
We'll decide about using our name once we've seen a draft. Please don't
mention our bank or any of our customers.
```

### The brief and the settings

```
Customer: Client K, a heating and cooling installer.
What we did: from February 2026 we took over Client K's invoicing and
  receivables. Invoices now go out from the job-completion form in their
  field-service app on the day a job closes. Every Monday we send their
  office manager a list of late payers with a follow-up email drafted
  for each; the office manager sends them.
What we think changed: cash collected is up about 20%.
Case study for: owners of trades businesses, on our services page and
  sent by email to prospects.
Interviewee: operations director, Client K. Interviewer: our owner.
Length: 30 minutes. Today's date: 9 October 2026.
```
