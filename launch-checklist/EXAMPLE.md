# Launch checklist: an invented example

*Illustrative example with invented data.* Quillfern Bookkeeping is an invented 12-person bookkeeping firm; the people are named by role only, and the pages, counts, dates and prices below are all invented. No real firm, association or person is described. The owner is launching a fixed-fee service for restaurants. First come the inputs the owner supplied, in full, then the checklist the capability returned.

## The inputs

### 1. The launch brief, with the size answers

```
What we are launching: Fixed-fee monthly books for restaurants:
  bookkeeping, payroll records and a monthly report, for $650 a month
  per location. A new service page with a booking link.
Who it is for: owners of independent restaurants with one to three
  locations.
The one action: book a 20-minute fit call from the service page.
Launch day: Thursday 19 November 2026.
Today: Monday 9 November 2026.
Launch owner: the owner.
Approver for anything that goes outside the firm: the owner.
Working days: Monday to Friday. Office closed: Thursday 26 and
  Friday 27 November 2026.

Size answers:
  Reach: a part of the people we can reach (restaurant owners only).
  New or improvement: new.
  Changes what we sell or how it is bought: yes.
```

### 2. Your facts

```
We are Quillfern Bookkeeping, a 12-person bookkeeping firm.
What we sell: monthly bookkeeping and payroll records for small
  businesses, billed monthly.
Who buys: owners of businesses with 5 to 50 staff.
Proof we may show: we keep the books for 9 restaurants today. We may
  say that. We may not name them.
Terms the restaurant service must match: $650 a month per location.
  No setup fee. Cancel with 30 days' notice. No launch discount.
```

### 3. The team

```
Owner: owns the launch date; approves everything that goes outside the
  firm; writes everything that goes out under the firm's name; sends
  personal notes and replies from their own email.
Senior bookkeeper: knows the restaurant clients; reads pages and
  drafts as a fresh reader.
Office manager: runs the website and the email list tool; tests forms
  and booking links; keeps the enquiries sheet.
```


### 5. Where the launch stands

```

  price, what is included and the booking link. Has a section headed
  "Client story coming soon" with no text under it.
Booking link: set up by the office manager. Not yet tested.
Monthly report sample: one page, finished, linked from the service page.
Owner's note: three restaurant clients asked this year whether we
  could charge one fixed monthly fee.
```

### 6. Hosts' dates

```
Restaurant owners' association newsletter: the editor needs our piece
  by Monday 16 November. The next issue goes out on Monday 23 November.
```

### 7. Earlier launches

```

  current clients and the newsletter list.
```

### 8. Existing material

```

  month."
Services page (live): lists bookkeeping, payroll records and year-end
  packs.
Sales one-pager (PDF, handed to prospects): "We do not offer fixed
  fees."
```
