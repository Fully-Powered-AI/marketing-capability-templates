# Creator campaign plan: an invented example

*Illustrative example with invented data.* The business, the creators, the figures and the dates below are all invented. The business is an invented eight-person hot-sauce maker that sells from its own online store and is planning its first creator campaign. Its creator programme lead ran the plan for the marketing lead; the founder approves spend. First come the inputs the programme lead supplied, in full, then the plan the capability returned.

8. DECISIONS
1. Approve the plan's spend: base-case cash spend 651.44 (conservative 580.72) against the 2,000.00 budget, of which 510.00 is committed before any order. Decides: founder. Options: approve as drafted; approve without CR-03's fee row; send the plan back. Recommendation: approve without CR-03's fee row (decision 2), because CR-03 loses money in both cases at the 20 orders assumed and the other three rows carry no fee.
2. CR-03 Creator C's fee of 400.00. Decides: founder. Options: approve the fee; offer commission only at 10 percent; offer a lower fee; leave Creator C out. Recommendation: offer commission only, because the fee breaks even at 42 or 31 tracked orders against 20 assumed.
3. The timeline is 1 day short: the approval round ends 2026-12-02, after the window opens on 2026-12-01. Decides: marketing lead. Options: open the window on 2026-12-02 and re-run the plan; shorten the approval round from 7 days to 6; start with CR-01 Creator A, already agreed, and let the others post later in the window. Recommendation: open the window on 2026-12-02, which keeps the full 7-day approval round that R3 relies on.
4. CR-05 Creator E posts on podcasts, which the settings do not clear. Decides: marketing lead. Options: clear podcasts for this campaign and re-run, after which Creator E's 300.00 fee also needs the founder's approval; leave Creator E out. Recommendation: leave Creator E out of this campaign, because a second fee of 300.00 would be owed whatever the orders and no break-even can be worked until podcasts are cleared.
5. Whether creator codes combine with the holiday sitewide offer (10 percent off, 2026-12-05 to 2026-12-07). Decides: marketing lead. Options: codes combine, so an order on those days carries both discounts and the plan's per-order figures do not hold for it; codes do not combine, so a buyer may take the sitewide offer, leave the code unused and the order is not credited to a creator. Recommendation: none, because no input says how buyers would choose; decide before the M2 test orders.
6. Codes after the window. Decides: marketing lead. Options: end with the window on 2026-12-14; stay active. Recommendation: end on 2026-12-14, which keeps credited orders inside the plan's window and narrows the leak risk in R5.
