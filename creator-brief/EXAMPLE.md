# Creator brief: an invented example

*Illustrative example with invented data.* Saltbrook Candle Company, its products, its people, its three creators, their codes and the web address are all invented. First come the four inputs the brand's creator programme lead supplied, in full, then everything the workflow template returned: a run summary, two briefs and one creator stopped before any brief was written.

The example shows five things a reader should look for. A point the plan wanted that nobody has cleared stays out of the briefs and is listed for the founder. A line in the cleared list with no cleared-by mark ("Cotton wick.") does not count as cleared and appears in neither brief. A creator who has not signed gets a brief marked not ready, with each gap named. A creator on a platform the brand has not ruled on gets no brief at all. A roster note asking for a ready-made caption is recorded and not followed.

In the output below, the format's headings are shown in bold so that this page keeps a single title, and each line of the run summary and of the notes is its own paragraph. The wording is otherwise as the workflow template's output format sets it; the published trial record lists where the recorded run worded things differently.

### 1. Saltbrook's creator rules (kept by the head of marketing; statements cleared by the founder)

```
Brand: Saltbrook Candle Company
Countries the creators' audiences are in: United States, Canada
Disclosure wording set by, and decides whether it fits each country: founder

Cleared statements
- Ember No. 3 candle: "Poured from 100 percent soy wax." cleared by founder, 2026-10-14
- Ember No. 3 candle: "Burns for about 50 hours." cleared by founder, 2026-10-14
- Ember No. 3 candle: "Hand-poured in small batches." cleared by founder, 2026-10-14
- Ember No. 3 candle: "Cotton wick."
- Tidepool diffuser: "Scents a room for about eight weeks." cleared by founder, 2026-10-14

Forbidden statements
- "non-toxic": we have no test that supports it.
- Any claim that a scent improves sleep, mood or health, for example
  "helps you sleep": we have no evidence, and it reads as a health claim.
- Comparisons with a named brand: we do not compare ourselves by name.
- Results the creator has not had: every story must be the creator's own.

Platform rulings
- instagram: with conditions: show a lit candle only on a heat-safe surface, never unattended
- tiktok: yes
- youtube: undecided

Disclosure
- instagram: "Paid partnership with Saltbrook" | at the very start of the
  caption, before the "more" cut, with #ad; in a Reel, on screen and said
  aloud in the first three seconds; the platform's paid-partnership label
  turned on as well | spoken: this is a paid partnership with Saltbrook
- tiktok: "#ad Paid partnership with Saltbrook" | first line of the
  caption; said aloud in the first three seconds; the platform's
  branded-content setting turned on | spoken: this is a paid partnership
  with Saltbrook
- Words that do not count on their own: "#sp", "#collab", "thanks to Saltbrook"
- Days to fix a live post when asked: 2

Brand rules
- Redlines: no alcohol or smoking in shot; nothing aimed at children;
  nothing that mocks a person or a group
- Short name in a brief: Saltbrook
- Brand voice: calm, warm, a little dry
- Talking-point limit: 2
- We reply to drafts within: 3 working days
- A post stays up for at least: 30 days
- Contact role: creator programme lead
- Audience rule: none
- Platform rulings made by: founder
- Creator codes and links issued by: creator programme lead
- Signed agreements kept by: creator programme lead
```
