Fully Powered AI added the calendar dates counted from the event day and moved off closed days, an owner on every line, the shape of every outward and payment line, the owner's own figures for cost with nothing estimated by the assistant, the short note of your own facts as the only source for what is said, the rule that nobody is added to a mailing list without permission, and the rule that the assistant books, pays and sends nothing.
