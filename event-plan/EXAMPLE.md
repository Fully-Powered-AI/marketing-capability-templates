# Event plan: an invented example

*Illustrative example with invented data.* Tallowby IT is an invented 15-person IT support firm, and the Calderbrook Chamber of Commerce and its Harbourside Business Breakfast are invented too. The people are named by role only, and every count, date and price below is invented. No real firm, association or person is described. The founder has been accepted to speak at the chamber's December breakfast. First come the inputs the founder supplied, in full, then the plan the workflow template returned.

## The inputs

```

Run by: the Calderbrook Chamber of Commerce (the organiser).

  and attending the rest of the breakfast.
Talk title (accepted by the organiser): "Before your next laptop
  refresh: five checks for offices of 20 to 100 people"

  Friday 27 November 2026.

### 2. The goal

```

  at businesses of 20 to 100 people.

  2 follow-up meetings booked by Thursday 17 December 2026.

```

  businesses of 20 to 100 staff.

  owners and managers of local businesses." (organiser's email,
  2 November 2026). No breakdown by role.

```

### 4. Your facts

```
We are Tallowby IT, a 15-person IT support firm.
What we sell: monthly IT support for offices of 10 to 150 people, and
  one-off laptop refresh projects.
Who buys: office managers and owners.

  since 2022. We may say that. We may not name them.
The next step we offer: a free 30-minute laptop check call, booked by
  email.
Terms: monthly support from $45 per person per month. No event
  discount.
```
