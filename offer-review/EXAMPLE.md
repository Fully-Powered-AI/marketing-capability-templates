# Offer review: an invented example

*Illustrative example with invented data.* Quillmere Bookkeeping is an invented firm, and its offer, its notes and every objection quoted below are invented; no real firm or person is described. The firm's owner packages a catch-up service for businesses whose books have fallen behind, and asks for a review before the page is rewritten.

The inputs come first, in full, so the template can be run on them. The review follows.

## The inputs

### The request

```
Please review our catch-up package before we update the page. We are
thinking of adding "Done in 10 days or it's free." I am the owner and I
check our terms myself.
```

### The offer as written (the services page, saved 2 November 2026)

```
The Books Back on Track Package

Behind on your books? We'll catch you up.

We reconcile every bank and card account for the months you're behind,
categorise every transaction, and set up a monthly close so you never
fall behind again.

What you get:
- All bank and card accounts reconciled up to last month end
- Transactions categorised in your accounting software
- A profit and loss statement and balance sheet for each month caught up
- A 45-minute walkthrough call
- Monthly bookkeeping setup

Price: $2,400 for up to six months behind. Each further month $350.

100% satisfaction guaranteed.

Limited spots — book now!
```

### Your facts (written by the owner)

```
Who buys: owners of service businesses with 5 to 30 staff whose books
  are 3 to 12 months behind, usually because the person who kept them
  left, or before a loan application or a tax deadline.
What they want it for: numbers they can hand to their accountant or
  lender, and to stop worrying about it.
Proof we can show: a sample month-end pack with invented figures, made
  last year; our written 14-step catch-up checklist. No client has
  agreed to be named.
How it runs: the client invites us as a user of their bank feeds and
  accounting software (we never ask for passwords) and sends statements
  for any account without a feed. We start the Monday after everything
  arrives. A typical catch-up takes 10 working days for six months
  behind.
What the client does: invite us; send missing statements; answer a list
  of questions on unclear transactions, usually 20 to 40, in one sitting
  of about an hour.
If something goes wrong: if the client's accountant finds a
  categorisation error in a month we caught up, we correct it at no
  charge within 60 days of handover. The handover includes a list of
  every transaction we could not categorise and why.
Real limits: two catch-up projects at a time; we have two senior
  bookkeepers. The next free slot starts 1 December.
After catch-up: monthly bookkeeping is a separate monthly fee, quoted
  after the catch-up. It is not included in the $2,400.
Price: $2,400 for up to six months behind; $350 for each further
  month.
Terms: 50% on booking, 50% at handover. No refunds. The "satisfaction
  guaranteed" line was written by our old web designer and is not in
  our terms.
Owner role: the owner. Terms owner: the owner.
```

No buyers' own descriptions of the offer were supplied. The review was run on 9 November 2026.
